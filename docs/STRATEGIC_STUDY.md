# Fresh strategic action study

This stage tests whether a learned action-conditioned strategic correction improves complete-root action choice beyond exact material and the engine's static Tapered evaluator. It does not establish playing strength or a Lichess rating. Previous search-teacher/value-study inputs are spent: their canonical feature identities are excluded rather than reused for tuning.

The planned workload is 30 runs: square/relation/plan encoders, five controls, two initialization/sample seeds, width16/depth1, 1024 optimizer steps. The five controls are transported slots with material anchor, conditioned slots without movement with material anchor, action-blind learned correction with material anchor, transport with frozen encoder, and actual transport with zero anchor. All controls construct identical state keys and share initial common weights, zero CP readout, loss weights and resident sample streams per seed. The zero-anchor run measures reliance on the material prior. These are small research models, not candidate strong engines by assumption.

Root families contain positions from plies12/24 of fresh legal random walks. Admission partitions 96 training, 24 validation and 24 test families, rejecting whole lineages with a canonical root/current/successor-feature collision across splits or any previous consumed corpus. Repetition history remains exact simulator provenance rather than an input feature. Shared early, unobserved game ancestry is possible; schema1 omits repetition/fullmove and clips halfmove. Split admission examines positions for collisions but does not generate test score labels. A separate disposable timing-pilot lineage is also excluded from the main corpus.

The producer builds complete breadth-first depth3 classical Tapered trees, evaluates the frontier and reduces every level. Root-to-child exports provide the action and root observation. Child-to-grandchild exports provide the **child side-to-move score at remaining depth2**. They are joined by context UUID, arena epoch, exact node IDs and exact child board/rule state. Root depth3 score must equal the negative minimum child score; the native root action must attain that minimum. Every retained group includes all root legal actions. A whole root is omitted when any child is terminal, missing a score or in the mate band, and groups with fewer than two moves are excluded. Coverage and omissions are recorded. This deliberately leaves forced-terminal decision coverage open.

Prediction receives the root feature tensor and known legal root action only. Exact successor piece algebra, material and blocker-aware pseudoattack/effect covariates are declared handcrafted priors. Simulator successor observations enter a separate frozen initial-encoder target branch for training and a full-refresh diagnostic, never the prediction input. The target encoder does not update. This measures approximate action-sensitive latent transport with a fixed representation target, rather than a latent world model discovering chess rules.

The common objective is normalized CP SmoothL1 (scale800) with weights value1, informative same-material sibling score-difference0.5, normalized successor-latent refresh0.05 and sibling latent-difference0.05. Pair sampling uses eight broad root-uniform sibling pairs and eight informative same-material pairs with teacher gap at least100cp when such pairs exist: 32 rows then, 16 otherwise. All data, feature decode, prediction, target encoding, loss and backward stay resident. Producer admission may synchronize scalar status; reporting/checksums/checkpoints explicitly download cold diagnostic data or weights. Sparse compact replay stores observations; it does not retain another dense feature corpus.

Anti-collapse diagnostics include fixed-target refresh error/state variance and action separation, exact action-blind ties on same-material siblings, and a legal-action shuffle intervention which keeps the **true material anchor** while replacing only the learned residual. Scalar gap diagnostics use every eligible pair. Latent pair diagnostics use at most256 deterministic evenly spaced pairs to bound temporary memory. Static Tapered uses source-equivalent signed integer truncation and is independently checked across colors and special moves. Root ordering uses unrounded float child-mover CP; integer deployment ties and real search behavior remain separate evidence.

Before any timing/training, the predeclaration pins sources, ordered spent-report hashes, producer manifest and workload. The timing pilot uses only a separate declared lineage and 64 steps for each relation-encoder control; it cannot tune losses, heads or selection. Main validation selects a candidate only if its two-seed mean CP MAE **and** complete-root selected-action regret beat both material and static Tapered. Eligible candidates are ordered by regret, then MAE, then names. No candidate is a valid outcome. The selection record is atomically sealed before any test label production. All frozen final models are then evaluated once on test; test diagnostics cannot change the selection or cause more training.

Milestones use atomic whole-report replacement plus an append-only JSONL journal. Restricted strategic checkpoints retain configuration, source family/simulator construction provenance and fixed target state hash; they are a separate format from existing deployment checkpoints. No outcome/WDL calibration or rating evidence is claimed.

Execution and results are pending the frozen predeclaration and recovered-device checks. Evidence links and observed results will be added after the bounded run; an infrastructure interruption is reported separately from a model result.
